Why it feels different

It feels personal because it is.

Starline Studio is not a large company or a product network with layers between the idea and the final build. Everything is shaped by a two-person team, which means decisions stay close to the product, details get more care, and each app can keep a clearer point of view.

That smaller scale lets us move thoughtfully, protect user privacy, and keep refining the experience long after the first version ships.
