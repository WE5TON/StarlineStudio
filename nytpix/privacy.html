Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2025

In this document, "Nytpix", "we", or "us" refers to Ethan Weston and Steve Edwards trading as NytPix.app, based in the United Kingdom.

1. Information we collect

Nytpix is designed to minimize data collection. We do not require account creation to use the app.

2. Local app data

Watchlists, preferences, and related app data are stored locally on your device unless you choose to back them up through Apple services you control.

We may receive limited crash or diagnostic information through Apple to improve reliability. We do not sell personal data.

The app may display movie information from third-party services such as TMDb. Their own privacy practices apply to their services.
